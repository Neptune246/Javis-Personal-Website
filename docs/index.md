# Welcome to Javis' Knowledge Base

I am a physics graduate with a [2:1](Physics%20Note%20Figures\Degree%20Certificate.jpg){ target="_blank" } from Durham University with a strong interest in condensed matter physics (CMP). My coursework focused on CMP, Modern Atomic and Optical Physics, Statistical Physics, Nuclear and Particle Physics and Quantum Mechanics. Additionally, I completed two final-year projects: an advanced laboratory project and a bachelor's project in CMP. Summaries of these projects can be found in [Project](projects.md) section.

Following my undergraduate studies, I decided to pursue a more applied branch of physics: medical physics. I currently hold an offer for the January intake of the MSc Medical Physics programme at the University of Aberdeen. Medical physics is an interdisciplinary field that applies physical principles such as magnetism and nuclear physics to medicine, particularly in medical imaging and radiation oncology. Examples include the development of proton therapy, Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) and the use of iodine-131 ($I\text{-}131$) isotopes in nuclear medicine. Given the broad scope of medical physics and its diverse career opportunities, ranging from clinical practice to research and development (R&D), I aim to identify and narrow down my area of specialisation by the end of 2027.

Alongside my studies, I actively participate in various university societies to develop key transferable skills for my professional growth. For more details on my involvement, see [Clubs and Societies](clubs_and_societies.md) section.

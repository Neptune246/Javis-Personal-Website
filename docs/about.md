# About me

I am a physics graduate from Durham University with a strong interest in condensed matter physics (CMP). My coursework focused on CMP, Modern Atomic and Optical Physics, Statistical Physics, Nuclear and Particle Physics, and Quantum Mechanics. Additionally, I have completed two final-year projects: an advanced laboratory project and a bachelor's project in CMP. Summaries of these projects can be found [here](projects.md).

To build on my undergraduate theoretical foundation, I am furthering my studies with a master's in Medical Physics at the University of Aberdeen. Medical physics is an interdisciplinary field that applies physical principles such as magnetism and nuclear physics to medicine. Examples include the development of Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) and the use of iodine-131 ($I\text{-}131$) isotopes in nuclear medicine. Given my background in CMP and my focus on magnetism, my goal is to specialise in the research and development of MRI technology.

Alongside my studies, I actively participate in various university societies to develop key transferable skills for my professional growth. For more details on my involvement, see [here](clubs_and_societies.md).

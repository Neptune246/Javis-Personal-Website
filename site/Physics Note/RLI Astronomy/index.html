    Comparing the masses of Jupiter calculated from Io and Callisto

    [!note] Hypothesise that the discrepancies are from the tidal force and Jupiter oblateness. Both of which can be modified by Perturbation theory.

    Relativistic effect from the Sun and Saturn should also be considered at the end.

    • Assumption made:
    • Two body system and the masses are treated as points.

    [!info] 1. ##### Anne M. Hofmeister, Robert E. Criss, Everett M. Criss, Verified solutions for the gravitational attraction to an oblate spheroid: Implications for planet mass and satellite orbits, Planetary and Space Science, Volume 152, 2018, Pages 68-81, ISSN 0032-0633, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pss.2018.01.005. (This is for the Jupiter oblateness) - $\(\varphi_{\rm gen,ext} = -\frac{GMm}{s}\left[1 + \sum_{n=1}^\infty \sum_{k=0}^n \left(\frac{a_{\rm svs}}{s}\right)^n P_n^k(\sin \theta) (C_{nk} \cos k\lambda + S_{nk} \sin k\lambda)\right]\)$ This is the generalised form of gravitational potential that accounts oblateness (zonal term) and mass anomalies (teressel term) where M and a_ave are the central body mass and its mean equatorial radius; s, θ, and λ are the spherical orbital radius, latitude, and longitude of the satellite; P_nk are associated Legendre functions of the first kind; and C_nk and S_nk are numerical coefficients. For small longitude (for moons of Jupiter), the teressel term can be ignored. - Potential energy will affect the gravitational force - Worth mentioning that tidal forces will cause Jupiter to have more bulges. In long run, it could significantly affect the orbit as Io is moving closer to Jupiter now. 2. ##### Jack J. Lissauer, Carl D. Murray, CHAPTER 42 - Solar System Dynamics: Regular and Chaotic Motion, Editor(s): Lucy-Ann McFadden, Paul R. Weissman, Torrence V. Johnson, Encyclopedia of the Solar System (Second Edition), Academic Press, 2007, Pages 787-812, ISBN 9780120885893, https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-012088589-3/50046-3. $\(\Phi\left(r,\varphi,\theta\right) = -\frac{Gm}{r}\left[1 - \sum_{n=2}^\infty J_n P_n\left(\cos\theta\right) \left(\frac{R}{r}\right)^n\right]\)$

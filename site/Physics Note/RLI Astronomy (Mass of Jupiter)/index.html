    RLI Astronomy (Mass of Jupiter)

    Using the resonance of the Gallilean moons to predict the effect of Jupiter on the abnormal apsidal precession rate of Mercury modelled by Peturbation Theory

    [!Question:] - What is the significance of investigating the orbit resonance - How much does G.R. and the solar oblateness contribute to Mercury's abnormal apsidal precession? - If we are comparing the amount of the influences on Mercury's orbit i.e. how much solar oblateness, gravitational force from Jupiter or/and the Sun and GR, should we include the Sun's mass into the gravitational force formula? If yes, then refer to the 4th reference. If not, then refer to the 2nd reference.

    [!info] - Orbit resonance occurs when the orbiting bodies exert a regular, periodic gravitational influence on each other, usually because their orbiting periods are related by a ratio of integers - Orbit resonance can either stabilise a system or destabilise a system - Jupiter's perturbation can push Mercury into a secular orbit (type of orbital resonance between two bodies with synchronized precessional frequencies), increasing the eccentricity of the orbit

    [!hint] The resonance of the moon alters Jupiter's quadrupole, which will change its gravitational potential. This, in turn, will affects its gravitational influence on Mercury, i.e. perturbation. - The idea is Gallilean's moons -> Jupiter Quadrupole -> Apsidal precession (slow rotation of perihelion of a body around a massive body) of Mercury orbit (which is affected by general relativity, grvaitational perturbation and solar oblateness.)

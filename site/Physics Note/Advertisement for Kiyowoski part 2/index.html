    Camera angle points from the bottom side to her phone, showing her face and the back of the phone.

    1. As she is scrolling through the Bracelets and Bangles section, her attention is attracted by something on the screen

    Then, cut to: an over-shoulder shot showing very side of the head and the shoulder, showing the screen. Cut to the same bottom side angle again.

    1. She pulls out a pic of her desired bangles and/or bracelets. She scrolls to the right, showing a white background while the real product appears in her hand. Repeat this for 4 to 5 products. She then smiles and takes a glance at the rings in the display box.

    The camera angle changes to the over-shoulder shot and maintain it until the products are all shown. After the last one, change the camera angle back to the bottom side angle again, showing her smile and as she turns her head to the display rack. And then change the angle to follow her sight to the display rack.

    1. Ash takes a glance at the display rack that contains rings. She approaches and ask Dayang to take a jewelry box from the front display box to the white table and open the jewelry box to reveal the ring.

    The camera follows Dayang's back and cut to a close up video at the table, recording Dayang opening the box. And then cut to the half-orbiting shot. Change a ring whenever the orbit reaches its end. During the last ring, swing up as fast as possible

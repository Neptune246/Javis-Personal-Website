    Trapped Ion

    Here we extend our concept on Optical Frequency Metrology where we mentioned that there are two methods to make our fractional uncertainty smaller, that is:

    1. Make our measurement time longer
    2. Trap more particles

    The strategy here is to use ions which are easier to trap due to the strong Coulomb interaction and it is much stronger than the scattering force discussed in Light Forces and Laser Cooling.

    But Earnshaw's Theorem says a collection of point charges cannot be maintained in a stable stationary equilibrium configuration solely by the electrostatic interaction. It can only create saddle points.

    [!Solution] We create the stable equilibrium using time varying fields: i.e. we rotate the saddle points so that it 'effectively' creates a parabolic well.

    Paul Trap

    • Electric quadrupole trap

    The setup is shown above. Each electrode is connected to the one opposite to it and are connected to an a.c. voltage. The electrodes at the center are connected to d.c. voltage, allowing trapping of ions.

    Maths description

    The motion of an ion in a Paul trap along \(i\) axis can be described by Mathieu equation: $$ \frac{d^2 i}{dt^2} + \left(a_i + 2q_i \cos(\Omega_{s} t)\right) i = 0 $$

    \[ q_i = \frac{4V_{ac}}{\Omega_s^2 d_i^2} \left( \frac{q}{m} \right) \]

    $\(a_i = -\frac{8V_{dc}}{\Omega_s^2 d_i^2} \left( \frac{q}{m} \right)\)$ - \(V_{ac}\) = voltage applied to electrodes - \(\Omega_s\) = angular frequency of RF voltage - \(d_i\) is the dimension (length) of trap along \(i\) axis

    For a particular charge to mass ratio, only certain ranges of those parameters lead to stable trapping

    The trap is approximately harmonic with oscillation frequency: $$ \omega_{\text{trap},i} = \sqrt{\frac{q_i^2}{2} \Omega_{rf}^2 - a_i} $$

    Practical

    The trap is loaded by ionising atoms from a weak beam. The fast electrons leave the trap but not the ion

    We use Doppler cooling in Laser Cooling to cool the ions down and we need 1 red detuned laser because ion oscillates back and forth in trap.

    When the ions are cold, we can consider the trap as a Quantum Harmonic Oscillator

    where the \(v\) is vibrational states and the spacings will be the same for both ground and excited states as it only depends on \(q/m\).

    If the vibrational levels are 'resolved' i.e \(\omega_{trap} > \Gamma\), we can cool to the lowest vibrational state

    Bloch's law

    • Magnons are the reason why the Curie-Weiss law drops more rapidly at the start.

    Excitation of a single magnon will reduce the net magnetic moment by 1 unit. Therefore we need to work out the number of magnons created.

    \[n_{\text{magnon}} = \int \left[ \frac{1}{\exp\left(\frac{\hbar\omega}{kT}\right) - 1} \right] g_m(\hbar\omega) \, d(\hbar\omega)\]

    See Statistical Physics.

    From the quantisation we see last lecture:

    \[\hbar\omega = 4 J_{\text{ex}} S \left[1 - \cos(qa)\right] \propto q^2$$ for small $q$. Be reminded that $q$ is the momentum like $k$ for phonons. That means $$g(\varepsilon) \propto \sqrt{\varepsilon}\]
    \[\implies n_{\text{magnon}} \propto \left( \frac{kT}{\hbar} \right)^{3/2} \int_{0}^{\infty} \frac{\sqrt{x} \, dx}{e^x - 1} \propto T^{3/2} \]
